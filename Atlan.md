### Who We Are

Atlan is building the missing context layer for data and AI — helping enterprises close the AI value chasm. Today, 95% of AI pilots fail because AI systems don't understand the context behind data: what it means, how it's governed, and how it should be used.

Atlan connects to every part of the modern data and AI stack to unify this context into a single, shared layer that both humans and AI agents can rely on. With Atlan, teams can:

1. Discover, understand, and trust their data.  
2. Build and collaborate on a shared body of knowledge.  
3. Activate that context across analytics, operations, and AI workflows.

Trusted by global enterprises including Mastercard, Workday, General Motors, Unilever, Ralph Lauren, FOX, Nasdaq, and Medtronic — and backed by world-class investors including GIC, Insight Partners, Meritech, Peak XV, and Salesforce Ventures.

With 400+ Atlanians across 12+ countries, Atlan supports global enterprises representing over $10T in combined value.

### The Interview Process

If your profile is shortlisted, you will be invited to participate in two rounds of interviews conducted via Zoom. The full process is designed to be light on prep and heavy on real conversation — we are interested in how you actually think and build, not in rehearsed answers.

1. Technical Deep Dive, \~60 minutes:

   This round will focus on your technical capabilities, especially your approach to problem-solving and your experience working with AI in practical scenarios. **We will also discuss any personal or academic projects you have worked on. Please ensure you have access to these projects (code, demos, or documentation) during the conversation so the panel can review and discuss them with you.**

2. Culture Interview, \~45 minutes:

   This conversation is designed to understand how you think, build, and grow — not to test you on textbook knowledge. We will explore the kinds of problems that get you energized, how you collaborate with peers, and how you respond when an abstraction or approach you trusted turns out to be wrong.

   What we're looking for: self-awareness, honesty about what you don't know yet, and the instinct to bring others into your work early.

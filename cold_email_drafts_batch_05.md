# Cold Email Drafts - Batch 05

Research date: 2026-05-12

Companies covered:
1. NeoSOFT Cloud
2. TsecondAI
3. RemoteStar
4. Coralogix
5. Swif.ai

Cold-email rules used for this batch:
- Keep the first email short, plain text, and easy to skim.
- Open with a company-specific reason, not generic praise.
- Lead with proof: shipped systems, metrics, project links, or open-source work.
- Make one low-friction ask.
- Use formal careers/apply paths when the company has them.
- Do not use support/sales inboxes for hiring unless the note explicitly asks for routing.
- Do not leave local file paths in the sent email body.

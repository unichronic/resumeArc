# Cold Email Template - tl;dr + Direct Fit

Use this for early-stage startups where the goal is to sound direct, human, and specific without becoming over-polished.

## Structure

**Subject:** Engineering at `{Company}`  

tl;dr;

`{One short sincere paragraph showing you understand the company and why you care. Keep it direct. Mention the specific mission/product/workflow, then say what kind of engineering work you want to do there.}`

Hi,

I’m `{Name}`, a `{degree/year}` student at `{college}`. I’m reaching out to ask if there is room for a `{role}` intern at `{Company}`.

`{1 paragraph connecting the company’s work to the engineering shape: backend systems, data pipelines, tooling, reliability, infra, AI workflows, ops, compliance, etc.}`

My strongest relevant work:

- **`{Experience/project 1}`**: `{proof point with stack/result}`.
- **`{Experience/project 2}`**: `{proof point with stack/result}`.
- **`{Experience/project 3}`**: `{proof point with stack/result}`.

If useful, I’d be glad to work on `{specific work areas}`. I’ve attached my resume.

Regards,  
`{Name}`  
`{Phone}`  
`{GitHub}`  
`{LinkedIn}`

## Notes

- Keep the tl;dr personal, not generic.
- Use 3-4 proof bullets, not a dense paragraph.
- Name specific company workflows instead of saying “I am passionate about your mission.”
- Admit domain gaps briefly only if useful, then pivot to the engineering fit.

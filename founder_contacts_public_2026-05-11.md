# Founder / Leader Public Contact Dossier

Research date: 2026-05-11

Scope: the 51 companies with generated tailored resumes in `tailored_resumes/full/`.

Rules used:
- LinkedIn is not used as an outreach route.
- No guessed email formats.
- No private personal emails, phone numbers, or scraped personal contact databases.
- Founder/leader emails are only listed when they appeared on an official page, official/partner PDF, YC launch post, public professional profile, or similarly public source.
- For mature companies, the useful target is often current leadership / recruiting / careers, not the original founder.

Cold-email rules used for this batch:
- Use formal application portals first for mature, enterprise, or structured companies.
- Use public recruiting emails only when they are explicitly listed for career opportunities.
- Do not use sales, demo, support, analyst-relations, or PR inboxes for jobs.
- Keep each note tied to the company’s current product surface and the matching tailored resume.
- Do not leave local file paths in the sent email/application body.

Important:
- This sheet only uses public business channels, company contact forms, careers pages, or public professional profiles.
- I am not listing scraped personal emails unless they were published by the company itself.
- For some companies, the best practical move is: founder/leader DM on LinkedIn or X plus a short company-form submission.

## Highest Priority Targets

### OpenRouter
- Best contact 1: `support@openrouter.ai`
- Best contact 2: Alex Atallah, Co-Founder & CEO, LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/alexatallah
- Best contact 3: Careers page / open roles: https://openrouter.ai/careers

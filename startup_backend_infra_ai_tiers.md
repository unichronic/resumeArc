# Backend / Infra / AI Engineering Target List

Based on:
- [startup_research.md](/home/unichronic/intern/startup_research.md)
- [startup_outreach_tiers.md](/home/unichronic/intern/startup_outreach_tiers.md)

This is a filtered version of the tier list for companies that plausibly fit:
- backend engineering
- infrastructure / platform engineering
- AI engineering
- inference / model-serving engineering
- MLOps / data-platform / observability / developer-infrastructure work

I excluded companies that read mostly as:
- consumer-only without a strong backend/platform angle
- services-heavy with weak product-engineering leverage
- domain-heavy roles where backend/infra/AI fit is not the obvious path

## 1. Research + Build a Proof of Work + Cold Mail

- OpenRouter: Strong fit for AI engineering, inference-adjacent platform work, API/backend systems, and model-routing infrastructure.
- Browser Use: Strong fit for AI agents, backend integrations, tooling, and developer-facing infrastructure.
- CodeRabbit: Strong fit for AI engineering, code intelligence, backend systems, and evaluation/review pipelines.
- SigNoz: Strong fit for backend, infra, observability, telemetry pipelines, and open-source devtool work.
- Pulse: Strong fit for AI engineering, document pipelines, backend systems, evals, and workflow automation.
- SubImage: Strong fit for backend/security infra, open-core tooling, graph/security workflows, and platform work.
- Dex: Strong fit for agentic product/backend work and workflow automation systems.
- Knowlify: Strong fit for AI engineering, media pipelines, backend APIs, and model/product integration work.
- Solidroad: Strong fit for AI application engineering, evaluation systems, backend tooling, and customer-support automation infrastructure.

## 2. Tailor Resume + Cold Mail

- Mintlify: Good fit for backend, developer tooling, docs/search infrastructure, and AI-assisted devtool work.
- Round Treasury: Good fit for backend/product engineering, workflow systems, and fintech platform work.
- Auctor: Good fit for backend/product engineering and enterprise workflow automation.
- SID: Good fit for retrieval, backend platform work, and AI infrastructure if your profile is strong.
- Swif.ai: Good fit for backend, device/platform security, and systems-oriented engineering.
- Novaflow: Good fit only if you have backend/data-platform or bioinformatics-adjacent engineering credibility.
- TsecondAI: Good fit for AI systems, edge/inference-adjacent engineering, and deeptech platform roles.
- Digital Paani: Good fit for backend/data/IoT platform work more than general product engineering.
- Atlas: Good fit for backend/product systems if they begin hiring more clearly.
- Work On Grid: Good fit for backend, infra, data-platform, and utility-tech systems work.
- Deccan AI: Good fit for applied AI engineering, data infrastructure, model operations, and platform work.
- CAST AI: Strong fit for infra/platform, Kubernetes, cloud cost automation, and systems engineering.
- ClickHouse: Strong fit for backend, databases, infra, data systems, and distributed systems roles.
- Chainguard: Strong fit for platform/security infra, build systems, supply-chain tooling, and cloud-native engineering.
- CockroachDB: Strong fit for distributed systems, databases, backend infrastructure, and storage/platform work.
- Dragonfly: Strong fit for backend, caching, infra, distributed systems, and database/platform roles.
- Upwind: Strong fit for cloud security, runtime infra, backend/platform, and cloud-native systems work.
- One2N: Strong fit for backend, SRE, DevOps, platform engineering, and infrastructure delivery.
- Coralogix: Strong fit for observability, telemetry/backend systems, infra, and platform engineering.
- Grafana Labs: Strong fit for observability, open-source tooling, backend, platform, and infra work.
- Kong: Strong fit for API platforms, gateway/backend systems, platform engineering, and infra work.
- TiDB: Strong fit for databases, backend infrastructure, distributed systems, and platform engineering.
- YugabyteDB: Strong fit for distributed systems, backend infra, cloud-native databases, and platform work.
- NeoSOFT Cloud: Possible fit for backend/cloud/platform roles, though the company is more services-led.
- TestMu AI: Good fit for backend/platform, test infra, AI-assisted QA systems, and developer-tooling engineering.
- OceanBase: Strong fit for database/platform/backend infrastructure roles.
- Apica: Good fit for observability, monitoring, backend systems, and platform/infrastructure work.
- Azul: Good fit for JVM/platform/backend performance engineering.
- NeoDocs: Possible fit for backend/product/data work, though less directly infra-oriented than the names above.
- Repello AI: Strong fit for AI security, runtime protection, red teaming, and backend/platform work around agentic systems, but current public hiring evidence is thinner than the Tier 1 names.
- HackerRank: Good fit for backend/platform engineering, AI-assisted developer tooling, and evaluation systems, but it is already structured enough for Tier 2.

## 3. Cold Mail Only / Shoot Your Shot

- Redis: Obvious backend/infra fit, but too mature for startup-style heavy outreach unless you already have strong OSS/database credibility.
- ManageEngine: Obvious backend/infra/observability fit, but hiring is structured enough that speculative outreach has low marginal payoff.
- Concerto by Trianz: Cloud/data/platform work exists, but it reads more like enterprise services than a product-engineering startup target.
- Xurrent: ITSM/backend platform fit is plausible, but the company is older and not a high-leverage outreach target.
- IBM Cloudability: Real infra/cloud-cost domain fit, but this is inside a large structured portfolio.
- StackGen: Strong infra angle in theory, but public hiring/team-shape evidence is still too sparse.
- CloudThat Technologies Pvt. Ltd.: Cloud/platform relevance exists, but it is training/consulting-led rather than a pure product-engineering target.
- Cloud.in: Cloud/backend fit is plausible, but current public metadata is too thin.
- i2k2 Networks Pvt. Limited: Infra/cloud relevance exists, but this reads more like a standard managed-services hiring flow.
- Miniorange: IAM/security/backend relevance is real, but the company is too established for deep speculative outreach to pay off well.
- Shellkode: Cloud/data/AI consulting relevance exists, but services-led structure lowers the payoff.
- RemoteStar: Broad cloud/backend relevance exists through remote engineering placements, but it reads more like a hiring/services platform than a product-engineering startup target.

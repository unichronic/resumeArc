# Cold Email Drafts - Batch 06

Research date: 2026-05-12

Companies covered:
1. CAST AI
2. Chainguard
3. Dragonfly
4. Mintlify
5. Novaflow

Cold-email rules used for this batch:
- Use formal careers/application routes first for companies with structured hiring.
- Keep any routing note short and technical.
- Open with a specific product/workflow reason, not generic admiration.
- Lead with proof: shipped systems, metrics, project links, or open-source work.
- Do not use security, press, sales, or support inboxes for jobs.
- Do not leave local file paths in the sent email body.
